TikTok Lead Collector Pro

Download the browser extension for saving qualified TikTok creators into your lead sheet while browsing TikTok manually.

Download Extension Check Backend
Version 1.0.0 · Chrome / Microsoft Edge

What It Does

Collects TikTok creator profile links, follower counts, profile bio, qualification status, notes, and duplicate checks for your Google Sheet workflow.

Install Steps

  1. Download the ZIP.
  2. Extract the folder on your computer.
  3. Open edge://extensions or chrome://extensions.
  4. Turn on Developer mode.
  5. Click Load unpacked and choose the extracted extension folder.

Ready For

  • Manual TikTok lead browsing
  • Existing creator duplicate checks
  • Google Sheet lead saving
